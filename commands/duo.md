# /duo

Show the cached same-team record, win rate, and games for two tracked players.
Results show the selected game mode, including when no games match.

Usage: `/duo teammate:<user> [server] [username] [game_mode] [position:1-10]`

- The primary player defaults to your linked League account.
- `game_mode` filters cached matches; omitting it displays `All Games`.
- `position` selects the primary player from a slot in the lookup account's latest match.
