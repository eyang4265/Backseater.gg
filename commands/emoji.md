# `/emoji`

Explains the emojis used by the bot.

Usage: `/emoji` (no options).

The guide covers player markers (⭐, 👥, 😢, 🫂, 🤡), previous-game cues
(👤, 😈, 🔄), streaks (🔥, 🥶), pentakills (🖐️), promotions/demotions (🎉, 📉), display
menus, charts, item placeholders, Arena team colors, and original champion icons.
🖐️ appears beside a featured player's K/D/A when Riot reports at least one
pentakill in that completed match, alongside any streak or rank-change marker.
It applies to every League queue with available results, including Mayhem and Clash.
In Ranked Flex, four known players plus one unknown on a full team gives all five 🤡.
Known players include tracked accounts, linked teammates, and saved partymates.
This does not save the unknown player as a partymate.
Known-player markers take priority over previous-game cues. Group markers are
inferred from known players or observed games, not confirmed Riot party rosters.
Champion icons use the original named champion artwork, with plain text when
unavailable. Other game icons represent their named rank, item, rune, or summoner
spell.
