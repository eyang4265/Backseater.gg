# `/laning`

Compare a player's Gold, XP, and CS with their lane opponent in a completed match.
The first view shows 5, 10, and 15 minutes. Use **Next** to inspect later
five-minute checkpoints in groups of up to three, including **End** for the
final game state; **Previous** goes back.
The chart compares Gold and XP at the displayed checkpoints. Jungle has no lane
opponent and cannot be compared.

Use `/laning` for your linked account's latest match. Optional `username` accepts
a League or Discord username, `server` selects its region, `match_id` accepts a
match ID or recent-game number (1 is latest), and `position` selects a player by
match slot (1–10).
